# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **IntegratedML Flexible Model Integration Framework** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community focused on advancing database-integrated machine learning with **InterSystems IRIS** and **IntegratedML**.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

### 🤝 Professional Collaboration
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and technical approaches
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code, documentation, and ideas
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

### 🧠 Technical Excellence  
* Sharing knowledge generously, especially about IntegratedML patterns and best practices
* Helping newcomers understand database-integrated machine learning concepts
* Providing clear, actionable feedback on demos, code, and documentation
* Contributing high-quality examples that advance the field
* Celebrating diverse approaches to solving machine learning challenges

### 🌍 Inclusive Community Building
* Welcoming developers from all backgrounds and experience levels
* Supporting both enterprise users and individual learners
* Encouraging questions and creating safe learning environments
* Recognizing that IntegratedML serves diverse industries and use cases
* Promoting accessibility in our documentation and examples

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

### 🚫 Harassment and Discrimination
* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission

### 🚫 Disruptive Technical Behavior
* Deliberately introducing bugs, security vulnerabilities, or malicious code
* Spam or off-topic contributions that don't advance IntegratedML knowledge
* Repeatedly submitting low-quality contributions without improvement
* Disrupting productive technical discussions with irrelevant arguments

### 🚫 Professional Misconduct
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
* Claiming false expertise or credentials related to InterSystems technologies
* Violating intellectual property rights or license terms
* Misrepresenting the capabilities or limitations of the framework

## Community Responsibilities

### 👥 All Community Members
* **Respectful Communication**: Use welcoming and inclusive language in all interactions
* **Constructive Contributions**: Focus on building better solutions for database ML integration
* **Knowledge Sharing**: Help others learn IntegratedML concepts and best practices
* **Quality Standards**: Strive for excellence in code, documentation, and examples

### 🛠️ Contributors and Maintainers
* **Code Quality**: Ensure contributions meet professional standards for enterprise use
* **Documentation**: Provide clear, accurate documentation for all features and demos
* **Testing**: Validate contributions work across different environments and use cases
* **Responsiveness**: Respond to issues and pull requests in a timely, helpful manner

### 🏢 Enterprise and Professional Users
* **Responsible Disclosure**: Report security issues through appropriate channels
* **Accurate Reporting**: Provide detailed, accurate information when reporting bugs
* **Professional Standards**: Maintain enterprise-level professionalism in all interactions
* **Community Investment**: Give back to the community that supports your work

## Scope and Enforcement

### 📍 Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

* **GitHub Repository**: Issues, pull requests, discussions, and code reviews
* **Documentation**: README files, tutorials, API documentation, and examples  
* **Community Channels**: Any official communication channels or forums
* **Events**: Conferences, meetups, webinars, or presentations representing the project
* **Professional Representation**: When acting as a representative of the project

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

### ⚖️ Enforcement Process

#### Reporting Guidelines
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement through:

* **GitHub Issues**: For public technical disputes or conduct issues
* **GitHub Discussions**: For community-wide concerns requiring broader input
* **Direct Contact**: For sensitive matters requiring privacy

**Note**: As this is a community-driven project without dedicated support staff, enforcement will be handled by active maintainers and community leaders on a volunteer basis.

#### Response Process
Community leaders will:

1. **Acknowledge** receipt of reports within 48-72 hours when possible
2. **Investigate** the situation fairly and thoroughly
3. **Consult** with other maintainers for complex situations
4. **Respond** with appropriate and proportional actions

#### Enforcement Actions
Community leaders may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

* **Warning**: Private or public clarification about unacceptable behavior
* **Temporary Restriction**: Temporary ban from community spaces or specific privileges
* **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from all community spaces
* **Content Removal**: Deletion of problematic content or contributions

## Appeals Process

If you believe an enforcement action was taken in error:

1. **Submit an Appeal**: Contact community leaders with a clear explanation
2. **Provide Evidence**: Include relevant information supporting your position
3. **Allow Time**: Give the community time to review and respond
4. **Accept Decisions**: Understand that final decisions rest with project maintainers
